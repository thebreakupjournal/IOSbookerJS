iPhone setup guide

Bookmark the guide, test the flow, and arm the booking.

Use this page as the runbook for BC Parks. Step 1 saves the guide and copies the bookmarklet, step 2 checks availability with canooq, step 3 runs a test, step 4 sets the lead time, and step 5 arms the real booking.

Start setup
Estimated setup time 5-10 minutes
You need Safari + photos + the bookmarklet
01
Step one

Bookmark the guide and copy the loader

Open this page in Safari, tap the share sheet, and add it to bookmarks or favorites so you can get back to the runbook later. Then copy the bookmarklet link below and paste it into the URL field of the bookmark you will use on the BC Parks page.

Bookmarklet link
  1. Tap the share sheet button in Safari.
  2. Add this page to Bookmarks or Favorites.
  3. Copy the bookmarklet link and paste it into your BC Parks bookmark.
  4. Keep the bookmark icon so the guide is easy to find later.
02
Step two

Check availability before you test

Use the canooq pass parking checker to decide whether you are aiming for today, tomorrow, or the next day. In your example, Cheakamus is available today, so that is a good test target. Then open the BC Parks day-use registration page and choose Garibaldi for the test run.

Canooq checker
https://www.canooq.ca/tools/pass-parking-checker
BC Parks registration
https://reserve.bcparks.ca/dayuse/registration
  1. Check availability on the canooq checker.
  2. Open the BC Parks day-use registration page.
  3. Select Garibaldi for the test.
  4. Pick the Cheakamus parking pass if that is the available option you want to test.
03
Step three

Run a test and open the timings tab

Leave the names blank for the test, then press Test. The bookmarklet should prime the form, move to the next page, and save the timing results. When you open the bookmark again, the Timings tab should show the recorded runs.

1 Test mode Use it when you want to measure timing without submitting a real booking.
2 Timings tab Open the bookmark again to review the stored run history.
3 Blank names Leave first name, last name, and email blank for the test.
4 Photo slot Add a screenshot of the exact flow once you capture it.
Success looks like: The form advances, the timings are recorded, and the modal shows a timings view when you reopen the bookmark.
04
Step four

Set the lead time for the real booking

BC Parks opens the booking window 2 days before the reservation date at 7:00:00 AM PT. The lead time is just how early you want the bookmarklet to start getting ready before that exact moment.

Example

If your reservation is Sunday, Aug 23, 2026, the booking window opens Friday, Aug 21, 2026 at 7:00:00 AM PT.

Lead seconds

Set a small cushion so the script starts before 7:00:00 AM PT, then watch the timing tab.

  1. Decide the reservation date first.
  2. Set the release time to HH:MM:SS in the modal.
  3. Choose the lead seconds that starts the flow just before the window opens.
  4. Keep Safari visible and do not let the phone sleep before the booking opens.
05
Step five

Prime and arm the actual booking

Fill in your real first name, last name, email, and country of residence before the live run. Then set the booking date, park, pass, visit time, release time, and lead seconds. When you are ready, press Prime / arm.

1 Identity Enter your real name, email, and country of residence.
2 Timing Set release time in HH:MM:SS and keep the lead seconds tuned.
3 Phone awake Keep Safari open so the phone stays on through 7:00:00 AM PT.
4 Prompt If page 2 details are blank, the arm flow will ask before continuing.

Troubleshooting

Common problems

The guide is not showing the bookmark icon

Make sure the page is bookmarked from Safari and that the favicon links stay in the page head.

The test does not move to the next page

Confirm you opened the correct BC Parks reservation page and that the Cheakamus or Garibaldi test data is loaded before pressing Test.

The final booking is not ready at 7:00:00 AM PT

Keep Safari visible, disable auto-lock, and make sure the lead seconds are set before pressing Prime / arm.

I want to add more photos

Add more files to guide/assets/ and swap the placeholder blocks in the sections above. The layout is already set up for step images.
